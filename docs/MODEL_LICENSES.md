# SIS AI SWAP STUDIO — MODEL LICENSES & COMPLIANCE

## Policy on Pre-Trained AI Weights & Checkpoints

1. **Academic / Non-Commercial Models:**
   - `inswapper_128.onnx` / `inswapper_fp16.onnx`: Sourced under InsightFace Academic & Research terms. Explicit user confirmation is required upon model installation. The UI flags these checkpoints as `NON-COMMERCIAL / RESEARCH`.
   
2. **Open-Source Permissive Models:**
   - `GFPGAN v1.4`: Apache 2.0 License. Permitted for broader use.
   - `Real-ESRGAN`: BSD-3 Clause License. Permitted for commercial & non-commercial use with attribution.
   - `SCRFD (InsightFace)`: Apache 2.0 / InsightFace terms depending on checkpoint.

3. **User Consent & Biometric Protection:**
   - Facial swapping and identity synthesis requires affirmative consent from the project creator.
   - Non-consensual deepfakes, illicit material, and unauthorized impersonation are blocked by policy in the terms and access logs.
